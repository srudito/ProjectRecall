Account Deletion

Delete your account in the app or request deletion without reinstalling it.

English Bahasa Indonesia Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Delete in the app

  1. Open Project Recall and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Follow the recent-authentication step, type DELETE, and confirm.
  5. Keep the device online until cloud deletion and local cleanup finish.

Deletion is permanent. The app removes eligible account access, profile data, owned projects and sessions, recordings, notes, bookmarks, timeline data, private cloud files, and linked identities.

Request deletion outside the app

You can request deletion even if the app is no longer installed. Email the support address below, preferably from the email associated with your Project Recall account.

Email an account deletion request

Support email: sulistyo.rudito@gmail.com

Include the account email and state that you want the Project Recall account permanently deleted. Do not send your password, authentication tokens, recordings, documents, or secret keys. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing the request. After verification, support will confirm the request and provide an estimated completion date. Shared-workspace resolution, unusually large storage scope, or legally required retention may require additional time.

Shared workspace or oversized accounts

Automatic deletion may be blocked when the account contains shared workspace relationships, content owned by another user, or an unusually large storage scope that cannot be deleted safely. Contact support so the ownership and deletion scope can be resolved without deleting another user's data.

Local copies on devices

An external request deletes the cloud account and associated eligible cloud data. To remove local copies from a phone or tablet that still has the app, complete the in-app cleanup, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Project Recall from that device.

Retention exceptions

Limited records may be retained only where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute handling, or backup integrity. Such records are not used for normal app operation and are removed or de-identified when the applicable purpose expires.

Penghapusan Akun

Hapus melalui aplikasi

  1. Buka Project Recall dan masuk.
  2. Buka Profile.
  3. Pilih Delete Account.
  4. Ikuti autentikasi ulang, ketik DELETE, lalu konfirmasi.
  5. Biarkan perangkat online sampai penghapusan cloud dan cleanup lokal selesai.

Penghapusan bersifat permanen dan menghapus akses akun, profil, proyek/sesi milik pengguna, rekaman, catatan, bookmark, timeline, file cloud privat, serta identitas terhubung yang memenuhi syarat.

Permintaan tanpa aplikasi

Anda tetap dapat meminta penghapusan meskipun aplikasi sudah tidak terpasang. Kirim email, sebaiknya dari alamat yang terkait dengan akun Project Recall.

Kirim permintaan penghapusan akun

Email dukungan: sulistyo.rudito@gmail.com

Sertakan email akun dan nyatakan bahwa Anda ingin menghapus akun Project Recall secara permanen. Jangan kirim kata sandi, token, rekaman, dokumen, atau secret key. Kami dapat meminta verifikasi yang wajar sebelum memproses permintaan. Setelah verifikasi, dukungan akan mengonfirmasi permintaan dan memberikan perkiraan tanggal penyelesaian. Penyelesaian workspace bersama, lingkup penyimpanan yang sangat besar, atau retensi yang diwajibkan hukum dapat memerlukan waktu tambahan.

Workspace bersama atau akun berukuran besar

Penghapusan otomatis dapat diblokir jika ada hubungan workspace bersama, konten milik pengguna lain, atau lingkup penyimpanan yang terlalu besar untuk dihapus secara aman. Hubungi dukungan agar lingkup penghapusan dapat diselesaikan tanpa menghapus data pengguna lain.

Salinan lokal pada perangkat

Permintaan eksternal menghapus akun cloud dan data cloud terkait yang memenuhi syarat. Untuk menghapus salinan lokal di perangkat, selesaikan cleanup dalam aplikasi, bersihkan data aplikasi, atau uninstall Project Recall dari perangkat tersebut.

Pengecualian retensi

Catatan terbatas dapat disimpan hanya jika diperlukan untuk keamanan, pencegahan penipuan, kepatuhan hukum, penyelesaian sengketa, atau integritas cadangan. Catatan tersebut akan dihapus atau dianonimkan saat tujuan yang berlaku berakhir.
