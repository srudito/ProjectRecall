Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 8 August 2026

English Bahasa Indonesia Terms of Service Account Deletion
Summary. Project Recall is a local-first recording and evidence-management app. It stores data on your device and, when you sign in and sync, in private Supabase services. Project Recall does not currently use advertising SDKs, does not sell personal data, and does not use AI processing or provide transcription in the current released version.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Project Recall mobile application and these public legal pages. Project Recall is operated by the developer identified in the app's store listing. Privacy questions may be sent to sulistyo.rudito@gmail.com.

2. Data we handle

Account and authentication data

  • Email address, account identifier, display name, app language, and account settings.
  • Email verification, password-recovery state, authentication-provider information, and password credentials used for email/password sign-in. Password credentials are sent to Supabase Auth for authentication and are not stored in plaintext by Project Recall.
  • If you use Google sign-in, Google and Supabase provide the identifiers and basic account information needed to authenticate or link your account. Project Recall does not receive your Google password.

Content you create or select

  • Workspace, project, and session names, descriptions, status, and timestamps.
  • Audio recordings you explicitly start.
  • Photos, videos, documents, and audio attachments you explicitly capture or select.
  • Notes, bookmarks, captions, timeline events, and recording offsets.
  • File and synchronization metadata such as file name, MIME type, size, duration, dimensions, private storage path, upload state, and error state.
  • Language and display preferences associated with your profile or sessions.

Device and technical data

  • Local SQLite records, app-specific files, settings, and authentication tokens stored using platform-protected storage.
  • Microphone, camera, and notification access only after the relevant feature and permission request. On Android, the system picker provides only the photos or videos you select.
  • Network-availability information used to schedule or retry synchronization.
  • Service providers may process IP addresses, timestamps, request metadata, and security logs when you authenticate, sync, open these legal pages, or contact support.

3. Why we use data

  • Create and secure your account and connected identities.
  • Record, organize, display, play, and synchronize your content.
  • Keep offline changes durable and retry failed synchronization safely.
  • Provide support, password recovery, security controls, and account deletion.
  • Prevent abuse, protect the service, diagnose failures, and comply with law.

4. Local storage and cloud synchronization

Project Recall is local-first. Metadata is stored in SQLite and files are stored in the app's private directory. When you are authenticated and synchronization is enabled, eligible content is sent to private Supabase database and storage services. Stable identifiers are used to avoid duplicates during retries. Cloud copies are protected by authentication, row-level access rules, and private storage policies. Project Recall does not claim that synchronization is a substitute for an independent backup.

5. Service providers and disclosures

We disclose data only as needed to operate the service or comply with law:

  • Supabase provides authentication, PostgreSQL database, private object storage, and server-side account-deletion functions.
  • Google processes information when you choose Google sign-in or link a Google identity. Google also hosts the support mailbox; if you email support, it may process your email address, message content, attachments, and delivery metadata.
  • GitHub Pages hosts these public legal pages and may log visitor IP addresses and request information for security and operation.
  • Authorities or other parties may receive data when required by applicable law, a valid legal request, or to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

Project Recall does not sell personal or sensitive data and does not currently include third-party advertising or behavioral-analytics SDKs.

6. International processing

Service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where applicable, we rely on provider safeguards and legal mechanisms intended to protect transferred data.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Local data remains until you delete it, clear app data, delete your account, or uninstall.
  • Cloud content remains until you delete the item or account, subject to safe retry handling.
  • Completed account deletion removes the active Auth account, profile, eligible owned workspace data, private cloud files, and connected identities. In-app deletion also performs scoped local cleanup on the device.
  • Automatic deletion may be blocked when shared workspace data cannot be deleted safely. In that case, contact support for assistance.
  • Limited security, fraud-prevention, legal, or backup records may be retained only where necessary and will be removed or de-identified when the applicable purpose expires.

See the Account Deletion page for in-app and external request methods.

8. Security

Project Recall uses HTTPS, platform-protected authentication storage, private Supabase Storage, row-level database controls, scoped synchronization, and restricted server credentials. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may:

  • Review account information and correct editable content through the app.
  • Delete sessions, content, or your entire account.
  • Revoke microphone, camera, photo-picker, or notification permissions in device settings.
  • Disconnect a linked Google identity when another safe sign-in method remains available.
  • Ask by email about access, correction, deletion, objection, or other privacy rights, including profile or account data that cannot be edited in the app.

10. Children

Project Recall is not directed to children under 13. If local law requires a higher minimum age to consent to data processing, users must meet that age or use the service only with valid authorization from a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised, and material changes may also be communicated in the app or store listing.

12. Contact

Privacy and support contact: sulistyo.rudito@gmail.com

Kebijakan Privasi

Berlaku dan terakhir diperbarui: 8 Agustus 2026

Ringkasan. Project Recall adalah aplikasi pencatatan rekaman dan pengelolaan bukti yang mengutamakan penyimpanan lokal. Data disimpan di perangkat dan, saat Anda masuk serta melakukan sinkronisasi, pada layanan privat Supabase. Project Recall saat ini tidak menggunakan SDK iklan, tidak menjual data pribadi, dan versi rilis saat ini tidak menggunakan AI atau menyediakan transkripsi.

1. Cakupan kebijakan

Kebijakan ini berlaku untuk aplikasi seluler Project Recall dan halaman hukum publik ini. Project Recall dioperasikan oleh pengembang yang tercantum pada listing aplikasi. Pertanyaan privasi dapat dikirim ke sulistyo.rudito@gmail.com.

2. Data yang kami tangani

Data akun dan autentikasi

  • Alamat email, ID akun, nama tampilan, bahasa aplikasi, dan pengaturan akun.
  • Verifikasi email, status pemulihan kata sandi, informasi penyedia autentikasi, dan kredensial kata sandi yang digunakan untuk masuk dengan email/kata sandi. Kredensial kata sandi dikirim ke Supabase Auth untuk autentikasi dan tidak disimpan sebagai teks biasa oleh Project Recall.
  • Jika Anda menggunakan Google, Google dan Supabase memberikan pengenal serta informasi akun dasar yang diperlukan untuk autentikasi atau pengaitan akun. Project Recall tidak menerima kata sandi Google Anda.

Konten yang Anda buat atau pilih

  • Nama, deskripsi, status, dan waktu workspace, proyek, serta sesi.
  • Rekaman audio yang Anda mulai secara sengaja.
  • Foto, video, dokumen, dan lampiran audio yang Anda ambil atau pilih.
  • Catatan, bookmark, caption, event timeline, dan posisi waktu rekaman.
  • Metadata file dan sinkronisasi seperti nama, tipe, ukuran, durasi, dimensi, jalur penyimpanan privat, status unggah, dan status error.
  • Preferensi bahasa dan tampilan yang terkait dengan profil atau sesi.

Data perangkat dan teknis

  • Data SQLite lokal, file privat aplikasi, pengaturan, dan token autentikasi yang dilindungi platform.
  • Akses mikrofon, kamera, dan notifikasi hanya setelah fitur dan izin terkait diminta. Di Android, pemilih sistem memberikan akses hanya ke foto atau video yang Anda pilih.
  • Informasi ketersediaan jaringan untuk menjadwalkan atau mengulang sinkronisasi.
  • Penyedia layanan dapat memproses alamat IP, waktu, metadata permintaan, dan log keamanan ketika Anda masuk, menyinkronkan, membuka halaman ini, atau menghubungi dukungan.

3. Tujuan penggunaan data

  • Membuat dan mengamankan akun serta identitas yang terhubung.
  • Merekam, mengatur, menampilkan, memutar, dan menyinkronkan konten.
  • Menjaga perubahan offline tetap tersimpan dan mengulang sinkronisasi secara aman.
  • Menyediakan dukungan, pemulihan kata sandi, keamanan, dan penghapusan akun.
  • Mencegah penyalahgunaan, melindungi layanan, mendiagnosis kegagalan, dan mematuhi hukum.

4. Penyimpanan lokal dan sinkronisasi cloud

Project Recall bersifat local-first. Metadata disimpan di SQLite dan file berada di direktori privat aplikasi. Saat Anda terautentikasi dan sinkronisasi aktif, konten yang memenuhi syarat dikirim ke database dan penyimpanan privat Supabase. Pengidentifikasi stabil digunakan untuk mencegah duplikasi saat percobaan ulang. Salinan cloud dilindungi autentikasi, aturan akses per baris, dan kebijakan penyimpanan privat. Sinkronisasi bukan pengganti cadangan independen.

5. Penyedia layanan dan pengungkapan

  • Supabase untuk autentikasi, PostgreSQL, penyimpanan privat, dan fungsi penghapusan akun.
  • Google saat Anda memilih masuk dengan Google atau mengaitkan identitas Google. Google juga mengelola kotak masuk dukungan; jika Anda mengirim email, Google dapat memproses alamat email, isi pesan, lampiran, dan metadata pengiriman.
  • GitHub Pages untuk halaman hukum publik dan pencatatan keamanan situs.
  • Pihak berwenang atau pihak lain jika diwajibkan hukum atau diperlukan untuk melindungi hak dan keamanan.

Project Recall tidak menjual data pribadi atau sensitif dan saat ini tidak menyertakan SDK iklan atau analitik perilaku pihak ketiga.

6. Pemrosesan lintas negara

Penyedia layanan dapat memproses data di negara selain negara Anda. Bila berlaku, kami mengandalkan perlindungan dan mekanisme hukum penyedia untuk melindungi data yang dipindahkan.

7. Retensi dan penghapusan

  • Data lokal tetap ada sampai Anda menghapusnya, membersihkan data aplikasi, menghapus akun, atau uninstall.
  • Konten cloud tetap ada sampai item atau akun dihapus, termasuk proses percobaan ulang yang aman.
  • Penghapusan akun yang selesai menghapus akun Auth aktif, profil, data workspace milik pengguna yang memenuhi syarat, file cloud privat, dan identitas terhubung. Penghapusan dalam aplikasi juga membersihkan data lokal secara terbatas pada akun tersebut.
  • Penghapusan otomatis dapat diblokir bila data workspace bersama tidak dapat dihapus dengan aman. Hubungi dukungan untuk bantuan.
  • Catatan keamanan, pencegahan penipuan, hukum, atau cadangan yang terbatas dapat disimpan hanya jika diperlukan dan akan dihapus atau dianonimkan setelah tujuannya berakhir.

Lihat halaman Penghapusan Akun.
